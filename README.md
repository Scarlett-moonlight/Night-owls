PROBLEM STATEMENT : Agricultural Market Intelligence & Dispatch Optimization ..!


**AGRILINK**: _Harvest more earn smarter_

One simple workspace to compare markets, plan dispatches and protect your Profits-built around the farmers.

FARMERS, in day-to-day life, faces many problems regarding the managment of crops. Firstly taking specific care of the crop, fertilizing it time to time. Also, using appropriate insecticides or pesticides on appropriate timings if needed. BUT STILL, After a lot of hardwork they still hassel to barely get enough profit on the yeild.


At some places market rates are quite good and on the other hand they are way to low in other markets. A simple farmer can never get to learn about the rate gap for their particular crops, the need or demand of that crop in particular market.
SO, HERE IS **AGRILINK**, a platform where farmers get to know about the recent need of market, the difference in the rates and can increase his net revenue at a good scale.


In The AGRILINK initially farmer registers himself and the crop he wants to sell in the market. Our argi market intelligence list's out all the markets(mandi) and displays the rate for that particular crop. Here, one can compare the rates of several markets, distance between the respective market markets and by selecting the preferrable mode of transport you can also calculate the burn that occurs in gross revenue due to transport. It also shows the gross revenue and the net revenue. This will definitely increase the gross income of a farmer.


AGRILINK also shows the DISPATCH OPTIMIZATION for the crops register. e.g. most of the crops registered on the AGRILINK are PERISHABLE vegetables ready to sell at market. So, our dispatch optimization analyses thee exipry for the crops and suggest possible dates when the crops/vegebles can be market ready. it also determines the distance between the selected market and your farm which again calculates the transport cost required, accurately.
